Gallery
*******

These images show the current development version of OpenRocket (26.xx-SNAPSHOT). The rockets are rendered directly by OpenRocket's Photo Studio, using the designs' own geometry, colors, and decals.

Rocket designs
==============

.. figure:: /img/showcase/atemis.jpg
   :alt: Atemis scale model with an orange core, two white boosters, and detailed decals.
   :width: 100%

   Atemis, from the supplied ``Atemis Original.ork`` design by Luis Ignoto Ledo. Its design notes credit a Kmobrain model as the basis for the modified upper and motor sections.

.. figure:: /img/showcase/vortikon.jpg
   :alt: Vortikon with white and gold decoration, pods, and elaborate fins.
   :width: 100%

   Vortikon, from the supplied ``vortikon-reset.ork`` design.

.. figure:: /img/showcase/parallel-boosters.jpg
   :alt: An unfinished rocket with two parallel boosters and visible wood-grain fins.
   :width: 100%

   The bundled **Parallel booster staging** example. Open it from :menuselection:`File --> Open example` to explore the component arrangement and staging settings.

.. figure:: /img/showcase/tube-fins.jpg
   :alt: A yellow rocket with a black nose cone and six tube fins.
   :width: 100%

   The bundled **Tube fin rocket** example, showing an alternative to conventional flat fins.

Interface themes
================

The same **Three stage low power rocket** example is shown in Light, Dark, and Dark High Contrast. The automatic theme setting chooses an appearance based on the operating system.

.. figure:: /img/showcase/themes.gif
   :alt: The OpenRocket design workspace cycling through Light, Dark, and Dark High Contrast, holding each theme for 2.6 seconds.
   :width: 100%

   Compare all three themes without changing the rocket or window layout.

For static previews and the theme setting, see :doc:`Preferences </setup/preferences>`.
